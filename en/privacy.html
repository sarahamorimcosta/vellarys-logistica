Privacy

Privacy policy

Updated November 2026.

Who we are

This is the website of Vellarys International Logistics, a fictional company created as a concept project. In a real project, this section lists the legal name, company registration number (CNPJ) and the data protection officer's contact.

What data we collect

The quote and careers forms store nothing on this site. When you submit, your browser opens WhatsApp with a message built from what you typed: name, company, origin, destination, mode, cargo type, weight and notes, or your job application details. You review the message before sending it.

How we use it

We use the data only to answer your quote, documentation request or job application. The legal basis is pre-contractual steps at the data subject's request (LGPD art. 7, V) and legitimate interest for the business contact you started.

Who we share it with

The message goes through WhatsApp, a Meta service subject to its own privacy policy. We do not sell or transfer your data. When the operation requires it, we share what is necessary with supply chain partners, such as carriers and customs brokers, solely to perform the contracted service.

Cookies and local storage

This site uses no advertising cookies or third-party trackers. We keep only two preferences in your browser's local storage: that you have seen the privacy notice, and the time of day chosen for the port scene. You can clear them at any time in your browser settings.

External services

To display the site, your browser loads fonts from Google Fonts and the three.js library from jsDelivr, which may log your IP address under their own policies.

Your rights

You may request confirmation, access, correction, anonymisation, portability or deletion of your data, and withdraw consent, through our sales WhatsApp. You may also lodge a complaint with Brazil's National Data Protection Authority (ANPD).

How long we keep it

Sales conversations are kept as long as needed to handle your request and meet legal obligations. Job applications are kept until the end of the selection process or until you ask for deletion.
