Location · Serra (ES)

Serra, where the routes cross

Vellarys is based in Serra, the industrial hub of Greater Vitória, close to the BR-101 highway and the ring road. Our trucks leave from here, our warehouse is here, and this is where the team follows every shipment in real time.

Get a quoteChat on WhatsApp

Close to the ports, away from city traffic

Serra is home to much of Greater Vitória's industry and distribution centres. From here we reach the Vitória, Tubarão and Praia Mole terminals and the airport without crossing the city centre, and join the BR-101 north and south.

It is also the natural starting point for the São Paulo and Belo Horizonte lanes.

What happens here

The Serra location houses the warehouse, the fleet yard, preventive vehicle maintenance and the control centre that follows trips and shipments. When you ask where your cargo is, the answer comes from here.
